# Last Bell Gym

A 3D sword-fighting career game that runs in the browser. Open `index.html` to play.

- Walk a brick-walled yard and teleport between training spots.
- Stand in the circle around a dummy to farm Power, Defense, Footwork, Conditioning, Composure or coins.
- Buy better swords and level them up on the training dummy.
- Duel five bosses in real time for the title.

Controls: W A S D to move, drag to look, E to interact, J or Space to slash. On a phone, drag the left half of the screen to move.
